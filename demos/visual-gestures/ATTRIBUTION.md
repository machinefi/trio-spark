The source footage is from **IPN Hand: A Video Dataset and Benchmark for Real-Time Continuous Hand Gesture Recognition** by Gibran Benitez-Garcia, Jesus Olivares-Mercado, Gabriel Sanchez-Perez, and Keiji Yanai.

- Dataset: https://gibranbenitez.github.io/IPN_Hand/
- Class examples: https://gibranbenitez.github.io/IPN_Hand/Classes
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Clips used without semantic relabeling: `c1.gif` (“Pointing with one finger”) and `c2.gif` (“Pointing with two fingers”)

The demo crops/scales the clips, repeats them to allow the decision panel to be read, and adds a panel containing recorded Trio-Spark production responses. It does not claim benchmark accuracy on IPN Hand.
