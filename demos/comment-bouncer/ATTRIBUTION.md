# Data attribution

- **Source:** [Civil Comments](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/civil_comments), test split
- **License:** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Adaptations:** a sample of 600 comments, 12–140 characters long, excluding links and political, religious and identity topics; 150 have a human toxicity score of 0.5 or higher, labelled `hide`, and the rest are labelled `show`

The usernames shown in the recording are invented for the demo; they are not the comment authors.
